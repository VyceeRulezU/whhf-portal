# Content & Voice Style Guide

Applies to all copy written for the site (headings, buttons, form labels,
error messages, emails) — not just marketing pages.

## Tone

Warm, dignified, and specific — this is a memorial-rooted organization, not
a generic charity template. Avoid both extremes: don't be somber/heavy on
every page (the site still needs to invite action), and don't slip into
upbeat "startup" energy that feels mismatched with the founding story.

- Lead with the person, not the process. "Help cover chemotherapy costs for
  a patient like Amaka" beats "Support our healthcare initiative."
- Be concrete about impact wherever possible: real numbers, real
  programmes, real outcomes — not vague "make a difference" language.
- Never use urgency tactics common to aggressive fundraising ("Only 2 hours
  left!", countdown timers) — WHHF's credibility rests on dignity, not
  pressure.

## Buttons & CTAs

Active voice, states exactly what happens: "Donate Now," "Send Your Gift,"
not "Submit" or "Click Here." A button's label and the resulting
confirmation should use matching language (button says "Donate Now" →
confirmation says "Your donation is confirmed," not "Submission received").

## Forms & errors

Errors state what went wrong and how to fix it, in a neutral voice — no
apologizing, no blaming the donor. "Enter a valid email address" not
"Oops! Something went wrong with your email."

## Sensitive content

Any content referencing Rev. (Mrs) Helen Okoye's passing, or (pending board
decision) Rev. William Okoye's passing, should be reviewed by the family/
board before publishing — this is not a copywriting decision to make
unilaterally. See `PRD.md` §10.

## Numbers & currency

Always show currency explicitly (₦ or NGN for naira, $ or USD for dollars —
never an unlabeled number). Round large impact numbers for headline use
("₦1.5M+ distributed to cancer patients") but keep exact figures available
in the admin/impact detail view.
